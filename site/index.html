Magpie

who is on, how far they have come, and what the wilds did today

A small collector runs beside your RuneScape: Dragonwilds dedicated server and reports who is online, how each character is getting on and what happens in the world. Your own site shows it live and keeps the history. Players install nothing.

Source on GitHub Open work

What the site shows

Who is online
Everyone on the server right now, by character name and platform, and everyone who has played, with their playtime and last visit.
Twelve skills
Agility to Woodcutting for every character, with the level and the progress to the next one, read from the world save on the game's own XP curve.
Quests and journal
Which quests each character has started and finished, and how much of the journal they have unlocked.
Weather and world events
The weather in each region, and the world's base raids, dragon events and hunts as they begin.
What happened
A feed of the day: deaths and revives, boss summons, landmarks found, first pickups, agility course times, and who came and went.
Chat, when you want it
Off unless an admin turns it on. Then the server's page carries the conversation for everyone who reads it.
An admin area
The collector's settings and the server's controls, with a stop that saves the world first, so nothing since the last autosave is lost.

How it runs

The collector

One small program beside the dedicated server. It reads the server's log and its world save and sends what it finds to your site, signed. Passwords, join codes, platform ids and addresses never leave the machine.

The events mod, optional

A UE4SS Lua script loaded into the dedicated server. It is a server modification, it only observes, and the game does not detect it. Without it Magpie still shows who is online, skills, quests, the journal, weather, deaths and saves. With it, it also sees chat, crafting, building, boss summons, landmarks, first pickups and agility times.

The site

A Docker image with a PostgreSQL database. Run it anywhere the collector can reach, on a machine of your own or a small cloud host.

What stays private

Public pages show character names and their platform, nothing more. IP addresses, platform ids, passwords and join codes never appear anywhere on the site, and chat stays off until an admin decides otherwise.

Free and open

MIT licensed. The source, the API contract, the collector and the events mod are public. Game facts come from the free dedicated server's own files, stamped with the build they were read from.
