04 / The hour in the wilds

The day and the hour,
still turning.

A dial rendered in Blender, with the sun by day, the moon by night and the time left until nightfall or dawn. It counts on from the world save at the game's own rate, so it keeps time on a rented server too, and holds the saved hour while the server is down.

The activity feed warns before nightfall and dawn, the day and hour follow you along the top of every page, and a stream overlay puts the dial and who is on over your game.

A small companion, a lasting record

Beside your server. There for your players.

01 / COLLECT

A quiet observer

A collector runs beside your dedicated server and reads its log and world save. It reports to your own site and keeps events on disk until they arrive.

02 / ENRICH

More of the story

The optional server mod adds chat, crafting, building and more world events. Chat stays off until an admin enables it.

03 / KEEP

A home for the history

Host the site and its database where you choose. Players only need a browser. Public pages keep addresses, platform IDs, passwords and join codes private.

A work in progress, with a working journal. Today, players, progression, the journal, the ledger, the map, activity, chat and world pages are available. The admin area covers settings, collector health and backups; server actions are not available in this version. Follow the open work.
